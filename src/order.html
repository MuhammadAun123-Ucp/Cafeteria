How to Order

Ordering at Campus Bites takes just four simple steps.

  1. Choose Your Items

    Browse the menu and deals pages or the boards at the counter and decide what you would like.

  2. Pay at the Counter

    Tell the cashier your order and pay in cash or by card. You will receive a receipt.

  3. Collect Your Order Token

    The cashier will give you a numbered token. Keep it with you until your food is ready.

  4. Pick Up Your Food

    Wait for your token number to be called or shown on the screen, then collect your order from the pick-up window.

Guidelines

Peak Hours

The cafeteria is busiest from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM. Order before 12:45 PM or after 2:15 PM to avoid long queues.

Pick-up Rules

  • Orders must be collected within 15 minutes.
  • A valid token is required to collect food.
  • Tokens are not refundable once the order is prepared.

Group Orders

For ten or more items, please order at least 30 minutes in advance at the counter.
